Blocksmith privacy policy

Draft for review — not yet effective.

Who we are

Sam Allen, trading as SAEVE Games, is the controller of personal information processed for Blocksmith. Contact saevegames@gmail.com.

Information we use

Blocksmith can be played offline. In the planned online-enabled release, optional online play uses an anonymous account identifier, your chosen nickname, Classic scores, Daily challenge results, nickname-rule acceptance, reports and blocks. Nicknames and ranked results are visible to other leaderboard players. Avoid using personal information in your nickname.

Game progress and session credentials are stored in app-private files on your device. Session credentials are not separately encrypted by the app. The planned release has no advertising or analytics integration.

Why we use information

We use online account and score information to provide requested leaderboard services, and moderation information to maintain fair and safe play. Proposed lawful bases: performance of the requested service and legitimate interests in security and moderation, subject to final review. Support enquiries are used to respond to your requests.

Service providers and security

Supabase provides authentication, leaderboard storage and account deletion services. Production requests use HTTPS. Supabase describes encryption at rest for hosted data. GitHub Pages hosts this website.

Before publication: confirm hosting regions, international transfer safeguards, provider logging, backups and website data processing.

Retention and deletion

Online accounts and leaderboard records have no automatic expiry. The tested candidate removes the account, nickname, scores and associated moderation and recovery-code records when account deletion succeeds. Local progress remains on your device. The planned release supports deletion through Privacy & Account in the app and through a one-time code on the website.

Before publication: confirm production deployment, external deletion URL, backup and log retention, and support-email retention.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing. Contact us at the email above. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We will update this policy when our processing changes, including before introducing advertising.
